Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

DetheAI ("we", "our", the "Service" at www.dethe.in) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture as provided by Google. If you sign in with email, we store the name and email you enter.

Content you create: The scripts and text you type to generate voiceovers are processed to produce your audio. Generation history is stored locally in your browser.

Payment information: Payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We never see or store your card numbers, UPI IDs or bank details.

Usage data: Basic technical data (browser type, device) may be processed to keep the Service running.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To generate the voiceovers you request
  • To maintain your credit balance and plan
  • To process payments through Razorpay
  • To provide support and improve the Service

3. Local Storage

Login details and remaining credits are saved in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Clearing your browser data signs you out.

4. Third-Party Services

We use: Google (sign-in), Razorpay (payments), and AI speech providers to generate audio. Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers listed above, or if required by law.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting us. Sign-in data stored in your browser can be removed anytime by clearing site data.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change when we do.
